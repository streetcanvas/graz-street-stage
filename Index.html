Our Story

Music
without
borders

♩
🇯🇵 Japan → 🇦🇹 Graz

We are Mitsuba Violin Trio — three violinists who left Japan and landed in Graz, Austria, one of Europe's great music cities. Mitsuba (三葉) means "three leaves" in Japanese: a clover, a symbol of luck, and the three voices we bring to every performance.

We began where music has always lived — on the street. No stage, no ticket, no barrier between us and the people who stopped to listen. From the cobblestones of Graz to private events, weddings, and concert halls, we carry that same open energy wherever we go.

Our repertoire refuses to be confined. We play Piazzolla and Bach, then shift into Studio Ghibli, then into the Les Misérables score — because music that moves people doesn't ask what genre it belongs to.
